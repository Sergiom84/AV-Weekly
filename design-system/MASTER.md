# AV Weekly design system

## Typography

- Titles and headings: Cormorant Garamond.
- Body, labels, controls, tables, metrics, and data: JetBrains Mono.
- Semantic hierarchy: title, subtitle, body. Body is the smallest tier.

## Composition

Keep data-heavy blocks isolated in components and reuse the existing Tailwind/Radix tokens. Tables and charts must remain legible in light and dark themes and at narrow widths.

## Content and accessibility

No emojis or explanatory microcopy in user-facing UI. Use accessible Lucide/Radix icons, visible focus states, keyboard navigation, and explicit loading, empty, error, and permission states.
